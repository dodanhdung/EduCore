This runbook is for an **isolated local/disposable** demonstration, not production. The reproducible automated path is the documented fresh-container R1b–R6 runners and the separate R8 scheduling/attendance runner in `R9_04_TEST_MATRIX.md`; R4's native suite creates its own synthetic principals and sources and drove real Edge→React→Nest→restricted PostgreSQL journeys. In the 27 October follow-up, standalone R8 passed 1/1 and full R4 passed 155/156, but its OTP timing acceptance still **FAILS**. These automated cases do not constitute a guided six-actor demo or an RC-ready verdict.

## Pilot monitoring and stop rule

Use a small named tenant and role list, daily review of failed jobs, OTP 429/503 patterns, audit/finance receipts and bug reports. Stop new financial writes and preserve logs/backup if any invoice total, wallet balance, payment count, approval state or idempotency receipt diverges. Suspend recovery access and pilot invitations if an external requester can repeatedly classify known accounts from response shape/timing within the enforced budget. Revoke the affected session/grant immediately on any cross-tenant or private-file access. Do not edit issued history to conceal an incident; use authorized reversal/adjustment and restore only under the recovery runbook.
